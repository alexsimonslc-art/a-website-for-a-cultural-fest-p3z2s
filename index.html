About Sanskriti 2026

Sanskriti 2026 is a grand annual tribute to India’s magnificent artistic heritage, where classical rhythms blend seamlessly with contemporary expressions. Over four spellbinding days, the Grand Amphitheater will transform into a living gallery of music, dance, visual masterworks, and culinary legacy. Join thousands of creators, maestros, and heritage lovers as we celebrate the timeless soul of our cultural landscape.

The Rhythms

Witness breathtaking classical Kathak gharanas and Sufi folk showcases that move the spirit and tell ancient tales.

The Flavors

Embark on a culinary journey mapping India's rich spice routes with masterclass sessions curated by heritage chefs.

The Crafts

Engage directly with indigenous craftspeople preserving generational techniques in live, interactive workshops.

Festival Schedule

Four days of breathtaking performances, culinary voyages, and masterclasses.

DAY 01 Oct 15
Aahvaan: The Grand Opening Ceremony
A mesmerizing lamp-lighting ceremony accompanied by 100 classical percussionists performing in absolute synchronization.
DAY 02 Oct 16
Kathak Beats & Sufi Mysticism
Elegant Kathak dance dramas during the daytime, transitioning into a timeless Sufi and Folk tribute under the night stars.
DAY 03 Oct 17
Sitar Fusion & Gastronomical Heritage
Live musical fusion workshops featuring sitar ensembles followed by tasting journeys exploring lost royal recipes of the subcontinent.
DAY 04 Oct 18
Milan: The Grand Finale Showcase
All featured master artists gather for an unprecedented cross-genre collaboration celebrating the unity in India's cultural tapestry.
